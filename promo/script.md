# SANCTUM — "PRESSURE" — Hero Script / Beat Sheet (v1, tone-approval draft)

> **VO CASTING REQUIREMENT (firm, from Lawrence):** All Sanctum promo narration is performed by a **Black male voiceover artist**. Previous Tiberious Productions promos used white voiceovers (incl. the edge-tts "Andrew" voice) and Lawrence did not feel it. We need presence, grit and authenticity. Delivery: **punchy, confident, aggressive, rhythmic. NOT soft. NOT overly scriptural.** Any TTS scratch for the animatic must be a matching Black male voice; the final is a real human artist. Full spec: `casting_notes.md`.

- **Runtime:** hero 0:39.4 (23 bars @ 140 BPM, bar = 1.714 s, beat = 0.429 s) · social cutdown 0:15
- **Aspect:** hero 16:9 master (2.39 letterbox inside 16:9) + 9:16 reframe; cutdown 9:16 first
- **Music:** 140 BPM dark trap / industrial, minor key (see treatment). Drop at 0:12.0.
- **UI:** real Sanctum captures (`app_captures/`), composited into stage plates. Latency number on screen ONLY from a real-device capture.
- Notation: `|` = beat, **[DROP]** = downbeat impact, *(breath)* = space left for the voice/music.

### Full VO text (hero, in order)
```
Room's quiet.
…not for long.
Sixteen pads. Eight banks. All muscle.
Touch it soft — it whispers.
Hit it hard…
…it answers.
You see it… before you hear it.
Lock the groove. Swing it how you feel it.
Your band. Your tempo. One clock.
Every millisecond — on the screen. No guessing.
From the sanctuary… to the main stage.
Sanctum.
Hit like you mean it.
```
(~60 words in 39 s: deliberately sparse so the voice can breathe on the drops.)
