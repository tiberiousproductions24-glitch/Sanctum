# SANCTUM Promo — Voiceover Casting Notes (v1)
**Project:** Sanctum "PRESSURE" hero (0:39) + social cutdown (0:15) · Tiberious Productions · Producer: Lawrence Davis Sr  
**Status:** spec only. No talent contacted, nothing posted.

## Casting requirement (firm, from Lawrence)
**All Sanctum promo narration is performed by a Black male voiceover artist.** This applies to the hero, the cutdown, lifts/versions, and any future Sanctum spots. Previous Tiberious Productions promos used white voiceovers (most recently the edge-tts `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` narrator on the Prayer Garden and Church Screen Reader promos) and Lawrence did not feel it. He wants a voice with **presence, grit, and authenticity** that matches the edgy, extravagant visuals.

> When posting the role, state it as a casting requirement for the character/brand voice (e.g. "Black male, 25–45, natural voice"). Self-identification by the talent is the basis; do not ask for photos to "verify."

## Vocal qualities
- **Register:** low-mid baritone. Weight in the chest; can drop to a near-whisper without losing body.
- **Texture:** natural gravel/grit. Not processed-sounding, not cartoon "movie trailer" bass.
- **Presence:** commanding, owns the room in two words. Sounds like the guy the band looks at before the count-in.
- **Authenticity:** natural cadence and inflection; street-confident but **premium** (luxury-sneaker-drop energy, not a radio-DJ read, not corporate).
- **Rhythm:** strong internal time. Must be able to land a word exactly on a snare and leave a clean gap on a drop. Musicians/MCs/drummers who VO are a plus.
- **Age read:** 25–45.

## Delivery: do's and don'ts
**Do**
- Short, percussive phrasing; hit consonants hard ("Lock the groove.").
- Swagger and a little smile on the cocky lines ("…not for long.", "You see it… before you hear it.").
- Play the gaps; let silence do work before "…it answers." / "One clock." / "Sanctum."
- Vary energy: near-whisper (shot 1, "it whispers") → full chest (shot 11, "to the main stage").
- Record to the music bed (headphones) so phrasing locks to 140 BPM.

**Don't**
- Soft, warm, "welcome home" church-announcer tone.
- Preacher cadence, sermon build-ups, or scriptural/King-James inflection.
- Over-produced trailer voice ("In a world…"), heavy fake gravel, or caricature.
- Rushed hype-man yelling; aggression should be controlled, not shouty.
- Upspeak or friendly questioning endings. Every line ends down and final.

## Reference vibe (styles only — no specific actors implied or claimed available)
1. **Sneaker / athletic launch spot:** low, intimate, cocky, every word placed on the beat.
2. **Hip-hop documentary narrator:** gravelly storyteller, unhurried, total authority.
3. **Arena hype intro / fight-night walkout:** controlled menace that opens up big on the last line.
4. **Late-night R&B radio host — but tougher:** smooth top, gravel underneath, premium.
5. **Producer talking shop in the studio:** conversational confidence on the tech lines ("Every millisecond — on the screen.").

## Budget & licensing considerations
- **Usage drives price, not length.** Non-union GVAA guide (2026) for digital/paid social & streaming: local ~$400–500 (3 mo) / $1,200–1,500 (12 mo); regional ~$525–750 / $1,500–1,800; national ~$1,000–1,250 / $2,700–3,200; worldwide ≈ 3× national. Source: GVAA Rate Guide — https://globalvoiceacademy.com/gvaa-rate-guide-2/ (summary via voicecrafters.com/industry-standard-voice-over-rates/).
- **Organic-only** (posted to Tiberious/Sanctum socials, website, app store video, no paid placement) is usually priced as non-broadcast/web: roughly $350–$600 for a short spot from mid-tier pros; Fiverr Pro often lower, but buy the **commercial use** rights explicitly.
- **Union:** SAG-AFTRA talent needs a signatory path (e.g. via a payroll/signatory company). Scale for a session is roughly $400–$620 before usage. Decide union vs. non-union before posting.
- **Get in writing:** media (organic social, paid social, YouTube pre-roll, app stores, website, live/stage screens), territory, term (suggest 12 months with renewal option), number of spots/lifts (hero + 15 s + 6 s bumpers), exclusivity (none or category-only), pickups/retakes included, raw + processed files, session direction.
- **AI/voice-clone clause:** contract must state the recording will **not** be used to train or clone a synthetic voice without separate written consent and payment.
- **Suggested working budget (to confirm):** $750–$1,500 non-union, 12-month organic + paid social US, hero + cutdown + 2 lifts, one directed session.

## Scratch / animatic VO
- If a synthetic/TTS scratch track is used for the animatic, it must be a **matching Black male voice** (low-mid, gritty, confident) so the tone being approved is the tone that ships. Do **not** reuse the edge-tts "Andrew" narrator from earlier promos.
- The scratch is for timing only and is labeled `SCRATCH — NOT FOR RELEASE`. **The final is always a real human Black male artist.**
- Better option if timing allows: book the artist early and record a quick paid scratch read on the bed; then the animatic is approved on the real voice.

## Audition sides (send with the 140 BPM bed + script context)
> Context: Sanctum is an MPC-style drum machine app for church and stage players. Dark, neon, industrial visuals; hard cuts on the beat. Read it like you own the room. Not soft. Not preachy.

```
1.  Touch it soft — it whispers.        (near-whisper, restrained)
    Hit it hard…                        (rising, hold it)
    …it answers.                        (full chest, lands after the drop)

2.  Your band. Your tempo.              (on the beat, clipped)
    One clock.                          (dry, final, let it hang)

3.  From the sanctuary… to the main stage.   (respect → open up big)

4.  Sanctum.  Hit like you mean it.     (one word, low; then a dare)
```
Ask for: 2 takes of each (one restrained, one pushed), slate name only, 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV, dry (no music, no reverb/compression baked in), plus one take recorded against the bed.
