# Sanctum Credits

## Audio
All built-in drum kits, Stage FX, church-bank loops, and **Sanctum Pulpit** tones
(organ, Rhodes, bass, pad, drum grooves, organ runs/slides) are **original synthesis**
by Tiberious Productions / Lawrence Davis Sr. No third-party sample packs. No Whoop
Triggerz® (or any other commercial backing-track) audio is included.

### Modern banks (v0.3.1)
**Trap Sanctuary, Drill Pulpit, Amapiano Praise, Neo-Soul Sunday, Stage FX Modern**: 80 pads,
**synthesized by Tiberious Productions / Sanctum** with `content/church-banks/_build/synth_modern.py`
(numpy/scipy, 48 kHz). They contain no samples, no recorded voices (the "choir" is formant synthesis),
and no audio downloaded or ripped from YouTube or anywhere else. CC0 audio: none used.
The genre research in `docs/modern-sound-research.md` is only a functional/sonic reference. No recording was used as a source.
The Pulpit **Modern 808** band mode is synthesized in-app (`src/pulpit/tones.ts`).
